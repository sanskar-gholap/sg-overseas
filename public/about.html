About SG Overseas

Your Gateway to Global Trade

SG Overseas is a premier agricultural export company dedicated to delivering the finest quality fresh and dehydrated products from India to the world. Founded by Mr. Sanskar Rajendra Gholap, we bring generations of farming expertise combined with modern export practices.

Based in Nashik, Maharashtra – the heart of India's agricultural belt, we have direct access to the freshest produce and established relationships with local farmers and cooperatives.

Our commitment to quality, sustainability, and customer satisfaction sets us apart in the global marketplace.

Our Mission

To provide premium agricultural products with transparency, reliability, and customer satisfaction while expanding globally.

Our Vision

To become one of India's most trusted agricultural export companies recognized worldwide for quality and professionalism.
