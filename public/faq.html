Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our products and services

What is your minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

Our Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ) depends on the specific agro-product requested. Typically, for bulk container exports like fresh onions or ground spices, it ranges from 1 Full Container Load (FCL). Please connect with our team on the Contact page for smaller tailored requirements.

Do you provide samples before bulk order?

Yes, SG Overseas provides product samples for quality verification before final commitments. While the samples themselves are free of cost in standard packages, international customs clearance and courier shipping charges are borne by the client.

What are your payment terms?

We work with standard global trade compliance options: 100% Irrevocable Letter of Credit (L/C) at sight, or an advance T/T (Telegraphic Transfer) split with balance payment settled against scanned shipping bills of lading (B/L) documents.

How do you ensure product quality?

Quality control is central to our exports. We source directly from trusted farm clusters and perform strict sorting/packaging checks. Additionally, we facilitate verified inspections through international agencies like SGS before dispatch to match dynamic global trade benchmarks.

What packaging options do you offer for agro exports?

We provide multiple high-grade packaging materials including Mesh/Leno bags for fresh onions, jute bags for grains, and multi-layer vacuum sealed packing for spices powder to preserve optimal freshness, aroma, and long shelf life during sea transit.

Which international ports do you ship from?

Most of our shipments originate from Jawaharlal Nehru Port (JNPT), Mumbai, which is well-connected for quick agro-product transit globally. However, based on regional availability and client preferences, logistics can be aligned through other major Indian ports.

Are your agricultural products organically certified?

We offer both premium conventional produce and certified organic categories for specific spices and pulses. All details, along with compliance certifications like APEDA or phytosanitary documents, are attached to the consignment file transparently.

How long does the delivery take after order confirmation?

Post advance payment or L/C confirmation, sorting and packaging take 7-10 working days. The ocean freight delivery duration varies purely depending on the target destination port country (typically 15 to 30 days).
