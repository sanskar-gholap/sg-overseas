About Founder

Mr. Sanskar Rajendra Gholap - Founder, SG OVERSEAS | Nasik, Maharashtra (India)

Rooted in a rich heritage of agriculture and trade, Mr. Sanskar Rajendra Gholap is the visionary founder of SG OVERSEAS. With over 7 years of dedicated experience in the Agri-products sector, Sanskar's journey began with hands-on experience at the grassroots level.

This deep connection to the land has given him an unparalleled understanding of the true strength and superior quality of Indian agricultural produce.

For the past seven years, Sanskar has been a vital player in the domestic market, consistently supplying premium-grade produce to several renowned exporters. This extensive background has equipped him with expertise that goes beyond just sourcing; he possesses a profound mastery of international packaging standards, complex logistics, and a precise understanding of global client requirements.

After years of contributing significantly to the export industry from the backend, he has now stepped into the global marketplace directly with his flagship brand, SG OVERSEAS.
